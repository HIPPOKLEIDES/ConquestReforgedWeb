---
title: ArdaCraft
---

<p>ArdaCraft is a server which seeks to recreate J.R.R. Tolkien&#x27;s Middle-earth exactly as it was described in The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit in one of the largest and most detailed Minecraft maps ever created. We have been building since November 2014, and already have well over 100 completed projects as varied as hobbit villages, elven cities, dwarven mines, human settlements and vast areas of detailed terrain. We focus especially on both geographical and historical realism in our builds, as well as using Conquest Reforged to push Minecraft to its full creative potential.</p><p>‍</p><p>ArdaCraft uses a few additional mods alongside Conquest Reforged, therefore you&#x27;ll need their modpack at <a href="https://modrinth.com/modpack/ardacraft/">https://modrinth.com/modpack/ardacraft/</a> in order to join.</p><p>‍</p>
