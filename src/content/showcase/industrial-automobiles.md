<p>A hulking eight-wheeled truck for heavy industry, a pleasant transport truck, and a light automobile, these three vehicles by xTechScammr push the limit in Conquest Reforged once again. For anyone interested in building modern builds, these will be a great source.</p>

<p>The first, a vehicle built specifically for rough artic terrain, can be dazing with the amount of blocks it uses, but it&#x27;s truly a sight to behold. This vehicle goes farther than most, as it seems to make careful use of invisible armor stands or item frames to make use of unorthodox diagonal models (eg: the tool racks). Even so, in most cases the build consists of plain, resourcefully employed blocks, such as the gothic window pane for the claw as well as branch and even bellows blocks for supports. </p>

<p>The second is simple, yet clean and elegant. Most blocks should be recognizable here. The third however, is much more complex despite its size. Here we see chairs, beams/railings, crates, benches and the unique wooden board model come together to make a small vehicle. If anything, this build goes to show the versatility offered by Conquest&#x27;s textures, as it manages to pull off what should be made of metal almost entirely with conventional wooden blocks. </p>
