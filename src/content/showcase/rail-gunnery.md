<p>For those more interested in brutal modern warfare and apocalyptic battlefields, xTechScammr&#x27;s third collection of builds offers a look at the sort of gunnery and firepower Conquest Reforged&#x27; blocks can achieve. </p>

<p>These three builds, which could probably be suitable as both armed railway trains or automobiles, naturally make use of the tricks developed by xTechScammrs previous two showcases, but expand into the territory of heavy weaponry. Gun barrels in all three are made with quarter blocks of various materials as well as metal pipe blocks. A particularly interesting detail is the use of a torch at the end of the barrel, perhaps signifying that the gun has just been fired! This gives the frozen nature of Minecraft builds a welcome sense of movement and action. The use of diagonal flat chairs for the supports of the organ guns on the second vehicle is also rather creative and works quite well, as it can take a few moments to realize that they even are chair blocks!</p>
