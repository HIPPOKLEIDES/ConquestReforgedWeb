<p>This collection of futuristic aircraft truly pushes the limits of Conquest Reforged, using blocks one would never think of being used for the heavy metal and machinery to great effect. Consisting of five vehicles, this collection made by xTechScammr is packed to the brim with dense detail and impressively maintains the graceful form and contours of aircraft.</p>

<p>The usage of painted shutters for metal engines, panels and turbines is particularly new, and adds a great deal to these builds with the various partially open and closed states that these blocks possess. While the idea of using tool racks and wooden railings might seem awkward, here they seem to work fairly well for rudders and other small diagonal shapes. Another clever detail we can see when looking closely at the second-to-last, red aircraft is the use of branch blocks and player heads for the wheels.</p>

<p>Perhaps opening a new genre of build for many Conquest builders, these builds surely will be a source for much inspiration. For other creative builders, hopefully the details and tricks used here be of some use no matter your theme of choice.</p>
