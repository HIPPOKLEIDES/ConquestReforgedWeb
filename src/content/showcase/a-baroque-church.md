<p>A fictional Baroque church, this build is impressive in its detail and subtle in it&#x27;s gradients and block choice. Constructed by Magicclippy, a German architecture student with an exceptional knowledge of historical styles, this unique work borrows from famous rotunda cathedrals, such as St. Peters in Rome and St. Pauls in London. The build essentially centers around the rotunda (ie: the dome in the middle) with symmetric classical temple facades decorating the entryways. </p>

<p>Overall, Magicclippy&#x27;s work here strikes a fine balance between capturing the ornate details required by the Baroque style and maintaining a level of simplicity that makes it so pleasant to the eye. It&#x27;ll surely provide plenty of examples for anyone constructing the columns, architraves, domes, and pediments found in buildings throughout the ages when using Conquest Reforged.</p>
