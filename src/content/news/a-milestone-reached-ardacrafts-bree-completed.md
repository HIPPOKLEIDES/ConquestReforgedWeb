<p>Our friends at ArdaCraft, the preeminent Conquest Reforged building team, have recently completed their latest project, the revamp and recreation of the town Bree from J.R.R. Tolkien&#x27;s Middle-earth. The completion of the project is a great feat for the ArdaCraft team, and the end result is a testament to their relentless research, planning, and will to chase the cutting-edge of modded building.</p><p>‍</p><figure class="w-richtext-align-center w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="/assets/66f339bc822de5699557ff26_66f338d10a578c9a6054714f_ac_bree_article_1.webp" loading="lazy"/></div><figcaption>A bird&#x27;s eye view of Bree</figcaption></figure><p>‍</p><p>Bree is a town in Middle-earth near the Shire, inhabited by men who mingled with hobbits, dwarves, and elves. It was a key location in the early part of the Lord of the Rings saga, serving as a stopping point for Frodo Baggins and his companions on their quest to destroy the One Ring.</p><p>‍</p><figure class="w-richtext-align-center w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="/assets/66f339bc822de5699557ff31_64b3137fe9db6e47d548d973_2.webp" loading="lazy"/></div><figcaption>The Prancing Pony</figcaption></figure><p>‍</p><p>The town itself boasts a large number of varied and detailed buildings, each with their own purpose and place within the village. You&#x27;ll find many great examples of medieval half-timber framed (aka Tudor) buildings that take advantage of the very latest models. Professions as varied as merchant, mason, carpenter and cartwright are represented, and aspects of settlements which many builds often ignore or forget - such as lavoirs (public place for washing clothes), breweries, graveyards and even middens - are included. The lore centerpiece of the village, the Prancing Pony, is built with painstaking accuracy to the books&#x27; lore. </p><p>‍</p><figure class="w-richtext-align-center w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="/assets/66f339bc822de5699557ff2a_66f3397f368da55421169202_ac_bree_article_3.webp" loading="lazy"/></div><figcaption>Windmill with Bree in the background</figcaption></figure><p>‍</p><p>Outside of the town lie breathtaking pastoral landscapes and farms, replete with only the best foliage from the <a href="/showcase/essential-trees">Essential Trees</a> pack. With the addition of the Create mod they&#x27;ve pushed their builds even further with an amazing windmill.</p><p>‍</p><figure class="w-richtext-align-center w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="/assets/66f339bc822de5699557ff34_64b3137fdac67d439862d6c2_12.webp" loading="lazy"/></div></figure><p>‍</p><p>For the full showcase, check out: <a href="/showcase/bree">/showcase/bree</a></p><p>If you&#x27;re interested in visiting Bree, you can learn more about ArdaCraft and how to install their custom CR modpack by visiting their website at: <a href="https://ardacraft.me/"><strong>https://ardacraft.me/</strong></a>. There is no download for the town itself, but their server does not have a whitelist and welcomes visitors from the public. </p><p>In the future, we hope to feature more news articles commemorating great milestones and project completions in the Conquest Reforged community. We hope you enjoyed this showcase of Bree, our first in a new trend!</p>
