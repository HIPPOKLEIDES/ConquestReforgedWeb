<figure class="w-richtext-align-center w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="/assets/66f34fd7408f5821c1af1e4c_66f34f75833a606516b50a48_refabricated_article_img.webp" loading="lazy"/></div></figure><h4>Conquest ReFabricated</h4><p>Conquest Reforged is now available for the Fabric mod-loader and Minecraft 1.19.2, rendering a whole new suite of mods available to our community! From the once familiar Replay Mod (now a Fabric exclusive) to powerful new performance enhancing mods, these days Fabric can offer Reforged players quite a bit to heighten their experience. Greatest of all is that Optifine is not required for connected textures, shaders, and other aesthetic features, making installation for players easier and our work as developers simpler. As this is the first release ever for Fabric, it will be marked as Beta, so if you encounter issues be sure to report them <a href="https://github.com/Conquest-Reforged/ConquestReforged">here</a>.</p><p>Worlds that are currently in Forge 1.18 seem to be fine when opened in Fabric 1.19 thus far. Still, always remember to <strong>backup your worlds</strong> before opening them in a new version!</p><p>We&#x27;ve also released <a href="https://modrinth.com/mod/ardagrass">ArdaGrass </a>(created by JayemCeekay from Ardacraft). It&#x27;s a Fabric alternative for Optifine&#x27;s &quot;Better Grass&quot; feature that also extends to modded grass blocks and nether &quot;grasses&quot; such as crimson and warped nylium.</p><h4><strong>How do we install it?</strong></h4><p>Without Optifine, we can package together a completely functional mod-pack on <a href="https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/conquest-reforged-official-modpack"><strong>Curseforge</strong> </a>for easy and quick installation, with no manual installations involved. The mod-pack will also be available on <a href="https://modrinth.com/modpack/conquest-reforged-modpack"><strong>Modrinth</strong></a>, and accessible through alternative launchers such as <a href="https://prismlauncher.org/"><strong>Prism</strong> </a>or <a href="https://atlauncher.com/downloads"><strong>ATlauncher</strong></a>. Personally, I prefer Prism and ATlauncher to Curseforge as they&#x27;re easier and faster to use. Just remember to set the game to use at least 8gb of RAM on these launchers!</p><figure class="w-richtext-align-center w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="/assets/66f34fd7408f5821c1af1e47_66f34fa80e579b653c6bd7aa_refabricated_article_logos.webp" loading="lazy"/></div></figure><p>Installation this simple was previously impossible since Optifine specifically prohibits its distribution on Curseforge and every other platform, forcing all players who depend on it to go to the Optifine website and risk installing a version that will cause crashes, break other mods, or just not even work with Forge. No longer does one have to go through this process or help their friends through it in order to play Conquest Reforged at its best.</p><h4><strong>What are the performance gains?</strong></h4><p>As you can see, performance gains can be substantial. With the mod-pack, my FPS has shot up from 55 FPS on Forge/Optifine to 145 FPS on Fabric (Chocapic shaders, middle of the forest, 16 chunk render distance).</p><figure class="w-richtext-align-center w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="/assets/66f34fd7408f5821c1af1e43_66f34fd29c63446bb867e654_refabricated_article_fps.webp" loading="lazy"/></div></figure><p>Minecraft 1.19.2 also seems to improve loading times a bit. Optifine hasn’t delivered much performance enhancement for quite a while and doesn’t show prospects of improving, so being able to access latest and greatest new performance mods on the block should prove exciting.</p><h4><strong>The Future</strong></h4><p>Given all the benefits of using Fabric and its more open and effective Optifine alternatives, development will probably be targeting Fabric more than Forge from here on out. Conquest’s Medieval armor and weapons mod should be ported to Fabric in time as well.</p><p>Many thanks to JayemCeeKay from ArdaCraft for pioneering the port of Conquest Reforged to Fabric. Your work has been a boon to Conquest Reforged and we look forward to working with you in the future!</p><p>‍</p>
