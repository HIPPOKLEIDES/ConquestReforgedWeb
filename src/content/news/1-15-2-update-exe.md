<h1>Welcome to the 1.15.2 Century</h1><figure style="max-width:1500px" class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"><div><img alt="" src="/assets/66f352e24367a3be3ff9d6b5_66f352d90eafa5a23d7ccb23_1.15.2_article_img.webp" loading="lazy"/></div></figure><h3>The moment we have all been anxiously waiting for is finally here.<br/></h3><p>We’re bringing Conquest Reforged 1.15.2 out of beta and into an official release! Fortunately, beta for Conquest Reforged has been rather stable, with the most troublesome issues being related to third-party mods conflicting with other third-party mods (eg: Journeymap vs Optifine chunk shenanigans). As such, all the bugs, mostly minor visual ones, that were reported on <em>Github</em> have been fixed, and a few additions have been made. Overall, not much has changed, and all the blocks from the beta remain intact.</p><p>There’s also an update for the Conquest Reforged 1.16.5 alpha. Additions and fixes from the 1.15 release have been merged into the 1.16 version, and the 1.16-specific  issues that have been reported thus far have been fixed and resolved. Generally, the alpha seems fairly stable, so I think it’s safe to say it’ll be coming out of alpha in time for or before SpongeForge’s much awaited 1.16 release. </p><p>On the team, we’ve been pretty busy in real life, so that’s slowed down development and this release significantly. Most of us have been working, and I’ve been pretty sick for the last few months. Nevertheless, we’re getting back into the swing of things and making progress once again. Along with the 1.15 and 1.16 update, the converter has seen a few fixes for 1.12-1.15. Right now  we’re actively working on a major facelift for the website to hopefully make it a more useful place for the community. So far, we’ve scrapped the old media page and replaced it with a curated Showcases page, where we show off and save some of the best Conquest Reforged builds. The header and footer have been replaced as well to be more in line with the <em>Conquest </em>aesthetic. Elsewhere throughout the site, pages are being edited to be more clear and informative. As for 1.17 on the horizon, my brief foray into the new version shows that it could be tricky due to a few factors, such as ForgeGradle updating and most of all Mojang reshuffling or renaming almost all of their files (again). Even so, it should not be anywhere near as time-consuming as the 1.15 update. </p><p><em>Note: If you’ve been using the launcher, you’ll need to go to the resourcepacks interface in-game and enable the latest resourcepack. We opted not to override the settings for which resourcepacks are loaded so that your own settings aren’t forcibly overridden. </em></p><p>‍<br/></p><h4><strong>1.15.2 Release Changelog:</strong></h4><p><strong>Additions:</strong></p><ul role="list"><li>Granite Embossment</li><li>Fancy Granite Dwarven Column</li><li>Fancy Granite Dwarven Design</li><li>Granite Dwarven Column</li><li>Granite Dwarven Design</li><li>Dark Rough Marble Dwarven Column</li><li>Dark Rough Marble Dwarven Design</li><li>Dark Rough Marble Dwarven Pillar 1</li><li>Dark Rough Marble Dwarven Pillar 2</li><li>Dark Rough Marble Dwarven Runes</li><li>Fancy Dark Rough Marble Dwarven Column</li><li>ancy Dark Rough Marble Dwarven Design</li><li>Fancy Dark Rough Marble Pillar</li><li>Leaded Glass Window</li><li>Gothic Sliding Window</li><li>Brown Oven Tiles</li><li>Green Oven Tiles</li><li>Gray Gothic Arch Frieze</li><li>Red Plaster with White Masonry Lines</li><li>Red White Floral Frieze</li><li>White Gray Floral Frieze</li><li>Glass shape variants for Cloud, Thick Cloud, and Thin Cloud </li><li>Light Gray Screen &amp; White Screen (for fancy screenshots and scenes)</li><li>Command block textures added as Circuit boards and screen, Iron and wood gear box, and Metal Gear Box</li><li>add granite wave stair and slab</li></ul><p><strong>Fixes:</strong></p><ul role="list"><li>Proper mirroring for various variants when using structure files (eg: better support for Terraforged, or whatever mod or builder uses structure files)</li><li>Fix Stump variant’s update method for Terraforged support</li><li>1.12 Glass culling for Cloud, Thick Cloud, and Thin Cloud readded (ie: for modern glass builds).
