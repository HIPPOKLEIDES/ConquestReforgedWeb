This pack is still in development, so many textures will be changed and added over time. Through some ad-links and the "Curse reward system"​, I get some compensation for the time I spend working on the project and keeping it free for everyone to enjoy.

That being said, I also offer an early access feature to everyone supporting my work by donating or becoming a patreon. So far, the lowest amount to get access to the tool is $5. However, due to future events and possible other tiers of rewards, this might change in the future.

Special Features

Thanks to Optifine and other mods to provide optifine features, Conquest_ has a lot of extra content you can enjoy without having to install big modpacks.

Items, armors, elytras and animals change their texture based on the name you give them using an anvil. Blocks also change textures based on the biome in which they are placed, giving you more ways to fancy up your homes.

‍Biomes can be changed via 3rd party tools like Worldedit in case the texture does not look the way you originally wanted.
