For servers and your own worlds

The Behaviour Pack

The Marketplace world comes with everything already switched on. To use the Historia blocks, decorations and citizens in your own singleplayer worlds or on a server, download the free add-on: the Historia: Rome behaviour pack together with the resource pack that holds its custom blocks, models and citizens.

  1. Download the .mcaddon file below.
  2. Open it. Minecraft imports both packs automatically.
  3. In your world's settings, activate the Historia: Rome pack under Behavior Packs; its resource pack is added with it.
  4. Servers: unzip the add-on, place the two folders in behavior_packs/ and resource_packs/, and add them to world_behavior_packs.json and world_resource_packs.json.

Questions

Where can I get Historia: Rome?

On the Minecraft Marketplace, inside Minecraft Bedrock Edition (Windows, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, iOS and Android).

Do I need the behaviour pack?

Not to play the Historia: Rome world. The add-on is only needed if you want the new blocks, decorations and citizens in other worlds or on a server.

Can I use it on Java Edition?

Historia: Rome is a Bedrock Edition product. For Java Edition, have a look at our free Conquest Reforged mod.
